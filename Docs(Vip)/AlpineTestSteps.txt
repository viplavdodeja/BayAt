J&J Alpine Assembly Bench Test Wizard

------------------------------------------------------- PREP -------------------------------------------------------
1. Connect assembly to test fixture. 
2. Make sure all tubing and fittings are connected and sealed.
3. Zero all sensors.

------------------------------------------------------- TEST 1 (CDA 1) -------------------------------------------------------
1. Turn on CDA and V4 switch.
2. wait 1 min for fill, and release CDA fitting.
3. Monitor values (QDF6, QDM14, QDF2, QDF4)
4. Wait 1 min for settle and record initial values.
5. Wait 1 min and record final values.
6. Release air and flip V21 switch to clear air in system.
	
Decay Tolerances:
	QDF6: <0.5 psi/min
	QDM14: <0.5 psi/min
	QDF2: <0.5 psi/min
	QDF4: <0.5 psi/min

------------------------------------------------------- Test 2 (CDA 2) -------------------------------------------------------
1. Turn on CDA and V4 switch. 
2. Toggle V26 switch and monitor QDM14. 
3. Confirm QDM14 goes to 0 when V26 is on.

------------------------------------------------------- Test 3  (CDA 3) -------------------------------------------------------
1. CDA on, and V4 on. Flip V21 on as well.
2. Wait 1 min for fill, and release CDA fitting.
3. Monitor values (QDF18, QDM7)
4. Wait 1 min for settle and record initial values.
5. Wait 1 min and record final values.
6. Release air and flip V21 switch to clear air in system.

Decay Tolerances:
	QDF18: <0.5 psi/min
	QDM7: <0.5 psi/min
	
------------------------------------------------------- Test 4  (ER1) -------------------------------------------------------
1. Turn on CDA, V4, and ER1 switches
2. Wait for number to settle, and record initial R5 value from CV5 sensor.
3. Calibrate if necessary. (Range: 2.50-3.75) (I always calibrate to 3.0 anyways)
4. Clear air and reset. (Turn on CDA, V4, and ER1 switches)
5. Verify R5 value has reflected changes from calibration.
6. Wait 1 min for fill, and release CDA fitting.
7. Monitor values (CV5, QDM5, QDF22)
8. Wait 1 min for settle and record initial values.
9. Wait 1 min and record final values.
10. Release air and flip V21 switch to clear air in system.

Decay Tolerances:
	CV5: <0.15 psi/min
	QDM5: <2 mmHg/min
	QDF22: <2 mmHg/min
	
-------------------------------------------------------Test 4.5 (CV3 and ER1) -------------------------------------------------------
1. Switch tubing fitting to CV5 sensor to 3-way valve.
2. Turn on CDA, V4, and ER1 switches
4. Monitor CV5 to check within range
5. Check CV3 release to verify air flow
6. Close and open valve to verify CV5 returns to stable state.
7. Release air and flip V21 switch to clear air in system.

Acceptable Range:
	0 psi < CV5 <1.20 psi

------------------------------------------------------- Test 5  (Vac 1) -------------------------------------------------------
1. Turn on Vac and V12 switches. Power on ER2 from 24 volt power supply.
2. Wait 1 min for fill, and flip Vac switch off.
3. Monitor values (QDF3)
4. Wait 1 min for settle and record initial values.
5. Wait 1 min and record final values.
6. Clear vac pressure by flipping V7 On and V12 off. Turn off power supply to ER2. Wait for pressure to equalize. Turn off V7.
7. Make sure vac is 0 (QDF3, QDM1, QDF19)

Decay Tolerances:
	QDF3: <5 mmHg/min

------------------------------------------------------- Test 6  (Vac 2) -------------------------------------------------------
1. Turn on Vac, V12, and V7 switches. Power on ER2 from 24 volt power supply.
2. Power ER2 with 1v, 3v, 5v, 8v, and 10v
3. Monitor sensor values (QDM1, QDF19) 
4. Wait for numbers to equalize and record value
5. Go through entire voltage walkup. return to 0v to ensure vac drops to 0.
6. Clear vac pressure by flipping V7 On and V12 off. Turn off power supply to ER2. Wait for pressure to equalize. Turn off V7.
7. Make sure vac is 0 (QDF3, QDM1, QDF19)

Decay Tolerances:
	for both QDM1 and QDF19:
		1v: -71 mmHg +/- 11
		3v: -225 mmHg +/- 11
		5v: -376 mmHg +/- 11
		8v: -603 mmHg +/- 11
		10v: (-674 mmHg - -760 mmHg) 


If unit passed all above, add part label sticker and Bench Test Pass Post it, and approve unit for console testing.
